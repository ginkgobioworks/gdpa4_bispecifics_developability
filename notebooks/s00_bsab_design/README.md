# s00 — Bispecific design coverage

Reproduces the design-space coverage analysis from the December 2025
bispecific selection notebook.  160 bispecifics were chosen from GDPa1's
246 mAbs via two strategies: bottom-up (FPS on mAbs, then pairing) and
top-down (FPS directly on the combinatorial bsAb space).  Selections are
recovered from the exported designs rather than re-running FPS.

See `manifest.yaml` for inputs, outputs, and governing decisions.
